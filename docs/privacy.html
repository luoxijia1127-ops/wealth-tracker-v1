1. 引言

欢迎使用 Nest（以下简称「本应用」）。Nest 是一款用于个人资产记录、净值展示与市场信息参考的工具类软件。本隐私政策说明：在您使用本应用时，我们可能如何处理信息，以及您所享有的相关选择。使用本应用即表示您已阅读并理解本政策。若您不同意本政策，请停止使用本应用。

2. 重要说明：非投资建议

本应用不构成任何形式的投资、财务或税务建议。本应用展示的行情、汇率及其他市场相关信息来自第三方数据源或公开接口，可能存在延迟、遗漏或不准确。您应自行判断并承担投资风险。

3. 账户与登录

当前版本不要求您注册或登录账户。我们不会因使用本应用而在我们的服务器上为您创建用户账号（除非我们日后推出相关功能并更新本政策）。

4. 我们如何在设备上保存信息

在默认情况下，您的资产与记账相关数据（例如资产条目、持仓与交易/余额流水、用于展示的净值或快照类数据等）主要保存在您的设备本地存储中。此外，本应用可能在本机保存应用偏好设置（例如默认展示货币、界面主题/配色等），以提供一致的使用体验。

请您了解：上述本地数据可能随应用卸载、系统清理或设备损坏而丢失；请您自行做好备份（例如使用本应用提供的导出能力，若可用）。

5. 网络连接与第三方服务

为实现行情、汇率、标的联想/映射等功能，本应用需要从您的设备向互联网上的第三方服务发起请求。此类请求通常由第三方根据其服务条款与隐私政策进行处理，并可能涉及例如：IP 地址、请求时间、以及您为搜索或查询而输入的代码、名称或关键词等。我们不会将您的完整本地账本内容上传至我们的服务器用于上述基础功能（在我们未提供云同步或账号体系的前提下）。

我们可能依赖的第三方数据来源类型包括但不限于：

  • 用于证券/基金等行情、K 线、联想搜索、基金净值等公开市场数据的接口服务；
  • 用于汇率等信息的公开接口服务（我们可能允许通过配置使用替代地址）；
  • 用于部分国际市场标的搜索/映射的服务；
  • 用于部分行情或历史价格的公开数据源；
  • 其他为实现功能所必需的公开网页或接口（以应用实际版本为准）。

我们不控制第三方的隐私实践。我们建议您阅读相关第三方的官方政策以了解更多信息。

6. 分析与广告追踪

在符合本应用当前版本实现的前提下，我们不将您的个人账本用于个性化广告；不将您的应用内数据出售给数据经纪人。若我们未来引入分析或崩溃统计等工具，我们将在本政策中更新说明，并在必要时根据平台要求征求同意。

7. 系统权限

在符合本应用当前版本实现的前提下，本应用不请求访问您的相机、相册、通讯录、精确地理位置等敏感权限（若未来功能需要，我们将更新本政策并在系统中说明用途）。

8. 未成年人

本应用并非面向 13 岁以下儿童。若您作为家长或监护人，认为儿童向我们提供了信息，请通过本政策末尾的联系方式与我们联系，我们将依适用法律处理。

9. 数据保留、删除与卸载

在您使用本应用期间，本地数据将保留在您的设备上，直至您在本应用内删除相关数据，或卸载本应用。卸载应用可能导致本地数据被系统清除且无法恢复。若未来我们提供账号或云同步功能，我们将在本政策中补充相关说明。

10. 安全

我们会采取合理措施保护应用与数据处理安全，但无法保证绝对安全。请您妥善保管设备、系统密码与备份。

11. 国际用户

若您位于不同司法辖区，您的权利可能受当地法律约束。您可以通过下方联系方式向我们提出与隐私相关的请求，我们将在适用法律允许的范围内予以处理。

12. 本政策的变更

我们可能不时更新本政策。当变更涉及重要内容时，我们将通过更新本页面日期和/或在应用内提示等方式告知。您在变更后继续使用本应用，即表示您接受更新后的政策。

1. Introduction

Welcome to Nest (the “App”). Nest is a utility app for personal asset tracking, net worth display, and market information reference. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the App and the choices available to you. By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Not investment advice

The App does not provide investment, financial, or tax advice. Market data, FX rates, and other information may be obtained from third-party sources or public APIs and may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate. You are solely responsible for your investment decisions.

3. Accounts and sign-in

The current version of the App does not require registration or sign-in. We do not create a user account on our servers solely because you use the App (unless we introduce such features in the future and update this Policy).

4. Information stored on your device

By default, your asset and bookkeeping-related data (such as asset entries, holdings, transaction/cash ledgers, and snapshot-related data used for display) is stored locally on your device. The App may also store preferences (such as default display currency and theme/color settings).

Please note: local data may be lost if you uninstall the App, clear storage, or if the device is damaged. Please maintain your own backups (including using export features if available).

5. Network connectivity and third-party services

To provide quotes, FX rates, symbol search/mapping, and related features, the App may send requests from your device to third-party services on the internet. Those services may process information such as IP address, request timestamps, and search queries (e.g., tickers or names you type). We do not upload your full local ledger to our servers for these basic features (as long as we do not offer cloud sync or an account system).

Categories of third-party sources may include, without limitation:

  • Market data services for quotes, candlesticks, search suggestions, fund NAVs, and related public market information;
  • FX information services (including configurable endpoints);
  • Search/mapping services for certain international instruments;
  • Public market data sources for quotes or historical prices;
  • Other public pages or APIs required for functionality (as implemented in each App version).

We do not control third-party privacy practices. We encourage you to review their official policies.

6. Analytics and advertising

Based on the current implementation, we do not use your personal ledger for personalized advertising and do not sell your in-app data to data brokers. If we introduce analytics or crash reporting in the future, we will update this Policy and obtain consent where required.

7. Permissions

Based on the current implementation, the App does not request sensitive permissions such as camera, photo library, contacts, or precise location (if future features require permissions, we will update this Policy and explain the purpose in the system prompt).

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us using the email below and we will handle the request in accordance with applicable laws.

9. Retention, deletion, and uninstall

Local data remains on your device until you delete it within the App or uninstall the App. Uninstalling may permanently remove local data. If we introduce accounts or cloud sync, we will update this Policy accordingly.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the App and related processing; however, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. Please protect your device, passcodes, and backups.

11. International users

If you are located in different jurisdictions, your rights may vary. You may contact us using the email below to submit privacy-related requests, subject to applicable law.

12. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. For material changes, we will update the Last updated date and/or provide notice in the App. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
